# Product Context: Ramen Nights & Budget Bites

## User Experience & Narrative Setting
The player steps into the shoes of an ambitious Tokyo Hodai University student enrolled in Economics 301. Alongside study partner Haruto (Engineering) and subsequent classmate recruits, the player turns a humble sidewalk noodle cart into an empire.

## Problem Domain & User Desires
- **Tactile Clicker Engagement**: Immediate visual feedback when serving bowls manually (floating Yen particle effects, sound effects, bowl interactions).
- **Idle Automation & Satisfaction**: Watching passive Yen-per-second (YPS) tick up steadily as classmates work shifts and kitchen gadgets automate preparation.
- **Strategic Depth**: Managing overhead expenses (daily district rent + staff salaries) against revenue streams, optimizing recipe selection for current weather and seasonal conditions.
- **Narrative Delights**: Interactive cutscenes triggered by game milestones and environmental events (e.g., rainy days, customer rushes, classmate breakthroughs).

## Core User Flows
1. **The Clicker Core**:
   - Tap the central Ramen Bowl in the Main Clicker pane.
   - Earn Yen based on active recipe base value, Academic Reputation multiplier, and active buffs.
   - Visual feedback via floating particle animations.
2. **Shop & Growth Loops**:
   - **Staff**: Recruit classmates to generate baseline passive YPS.
   - **Hardware**: Buy kitchen upgrades to multiply total kitchen efficiency exponentially.
   - **Menu**: Research higher-tier broth recipes to increase base bowl yields and unlock condition-specific multipliers.
   - **Consumables**: Buy temporary buffs (Energy Drink for 2x click power, Lucky Cat for +10% passive income, Staff Party for 2x staff speed).
3. **Ascension (District Expansion)**:
   - Save up required Yen and Academic Reputation to challenge the district boss and unlock the next area.
   - Soft-reset local shop progress in exchange for a permanent, compounding district Trophy (+50% YPS multiplier per trophy).
4. **Narrative & Progression Milestones**:
   - Complete procedurally verified quests for direct Rep rewards.
   - Unlock achievements and seasonal decor themes.
   - Read terminal activity logs in the side panel.

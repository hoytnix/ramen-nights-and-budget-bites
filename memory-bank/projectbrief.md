# Project Brief: Ramen Nights & Budget Bites

## Overview
**Ramen Nights & Budget Bites** is a JRPG-styled idle clicker and culinary management simulation set at Tokyo Hodai University. The player manages an entrepreneurial ramen stall as part of the eccentric Economics 301 course ("Applied Market Dynamics through Culinary Entrepreneurship") taught by Professor X.

## Core Objectives
1. **Graduation & Capstone Goal**: Expand the ramen stall across four increasingly prestigious Tokyo districts (Hongo Area, Shibuya Cross, Shinjuku Nights, Ginza Palace) and reach the Academic Reputation and wealth needed to graduate before the academic year ends.
2. **Dual-Currency Mastery**: Balance liquid operating cash (Yen) against long-term academic standing (Academic Reputation).
3. **Culinary R&D**: Unlock and master 20 distinct ramen broth recipes, ranging from student staple Classic Shoyu to mythical cosmic bowls like Void Broth and The Omni.
4. **Classmate Recruitment**: Hire and level up university classmates from different academic disciplines (Engineering, Chemistry, Art, Law, Math, Econ, Bio, Sports, CompSci, Tenure) to automate bowl preparation, boost passive income, and lower operational overhead.
5. **Kitchen & Atmosphere Evolution**: Upgrade culinary machinery (from basic burners to reality warpers) and unlock aesthetic themes that harmonize with real-time seasonal shifts.

## Scope & Constraints
- **Platform**: Offline-first, responsive single-page web app / Progressive Web App (PWA).
- **Aesthetic**: Retro 8-bit / 16-bit JRPG aesthetic, pixel art UI components, chiptune/scanline feel.
- **Client-Side Autonomy**: Zero backend dependency; all progression, narrative flags, and timers persist strictly in local client storage.
